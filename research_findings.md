**Research Summary – Chicken Pakoda (Pakora)**  

**Key Findings**  
- Traditional deep‑fried chicken pakoda remains a staple street‑food in India, but health‑conscious variations (air‑fryer, baked) are gaining traction.  
- Nutritional content varies widely: a typical air‑fryer recipe delivers **≈330 kcal** and **32 g protein** per serving, while a high‑protein video recipe lists **83 g protein, 30 g fat, 14 g carbs** for a larger batch. Standard deep‑fried pakoda (MealStack data) averages **590 kcal, 6 g protein, 61 g fat** per 100 g, with very high Vitamin E (≈390 % DV).

**Recent Developments**  
1. **Air‑fryer adaptation** – promotes lower fat while retaining crispness, aligning with the growing demand for healthier snack options.  
2. **Fast‑food market growth** – India’s fast‑food sector is projected to rise from **USD 19.9 bn (2025)** to **USD 37.6 bn by 2034** (CAGR ≈ 6.9 %). This expansion fuels increased commercial availability of ready‑to‑eat pakoda variants in malls and delivery platforms.
